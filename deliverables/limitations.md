# Limitations Note

## Data
- **The included job postings are synthetic.** The 320 postings in `code/data/demo_postings.csv` were generated by a script, including their skill frequencies and the rising trends. They demonstrate the pipeline and say nothing about the real job market.
- The Kaggle datasets named in the brief are not bundled. The app imports them when their CSV files are placed in `code/data/raw`.
- Public datasets may be old or skewed toward certain sites, cities and roles. Mentions in postings measure what employers wrote, not actual hiring.
- If the newest posting is over 365 days old, market claims are capped at Low confidence. If a dataset has no dates, trend claims are skipped and confidence is capped at Medium.

## Scoring
- The rubric is a transparent heuristic. It has not been validated against recruiter decisions or hiring outcomes, and it is not any employer's real ATS.
- Skill detection uses a fixed vocabulary of common skills. Unlisted skills are missed, and unusual wording can be misread. Coverage is strongest for technology roles.
- The role-match check uses a small synthetic set, so it is a sanity check and not an accuracy estimate.
- PDF text extraction can scramble multi-column resumes. Scanned PDFs are not supported.

## Decision tools
- The What-If Simulator estimates rubric changes. It does not edit the resume or predict an employer's decision.
- Roadmap priority weights and learning-time estimates are transparent design choices, not measured outcomes.
- Job matches compare listed skills only. With demo data, they are not real openings.
- Voice input depends on the browser's speech service and may send audio online. Transcription errors can affect feedback.

## AI output
- Gemini writes bullet rewrites, tailored questions, answer feedback and the report summary. Results can vary between runs.
- QA catches added skills, added numbers and unsupported company names by rule. It cannot prove that every sentence is correct or useful.
- Answer feedback is practice guidance. Without Gemini it is a rule-based estimate of structure and coverage, labelled Low confidence, and does not check technical correctness.

## System
- It is a local, single-student app with no login. Resume data is stored unencrypted in a local SQLite file.
- With AI enabled, resume and job text is sent to Google's Gemini API. Emails and phone numbers are removed first, but names and other details may remain.
- The readiness score is a planning aid with chosen weights. It does not predict placement.
- Each MCP call starts a short-lived local server process, which is simple but adds a few seconds to the weekly report.
